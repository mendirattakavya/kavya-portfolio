in the making

From client servicing internships to leading creator partnerships and brand collaborations for a mobility platform moving a whole city.

I started out with client servicing internships at Ogilvy & Mather and GoZoop, then moved through Natura and into Schbang Digital Solutions, leading strategy for brands like nurture.farm and Tata Consultancy Services.

From 2022 I was at 22Feet Tribal Worldwide, running cross platform strategy for BGMI, Flipkart Ads, Telstra, TTK Prestige and Pedigree, including the BGMI in game wedding that earned a Cannes Lions 2025 shortlist. Since March 2026, I've been Creative Manager at Namma Yatri, building the brand partnerships and creator ecosystem from scratch.
